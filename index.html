Draya_youtube

A private tool that uploads finished videos to a single YouTube channel.

What this application does

Draya_youtube is a small command-line tool used by one person to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It uploads a video file, sets its title and description, and reads back the upload status. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channel. The application signs in with that single Google Account and acts on no one else's behalf.

Google account data

The application asks for permission to upload videos to, and read data from, the signed-in YouTube channel. It stores the resulting access token on the owner's own computer and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.

AI disclosure

Draya is an AI-generated character. Every video published through this tool is labelled as AI-generated, both in its description and through YouTube's own synthetic-media flag.
